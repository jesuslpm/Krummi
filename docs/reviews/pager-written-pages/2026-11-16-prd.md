### R1 — Major: Q1's exception to I1 and AC-I1 is not declared

**Location:** Acceptance criteria / inherited AC-I1, and annotations to the integrity PRD: `docs/prd/krummi/pager/pager-written-pages.prd.md:116` and `:168`; decision Q1 at `:38`.

The draft preserves ancestor acceptance criteria except where it declares a replacement or clarification. Its integrity annotations clarify AC-I4, AC-I5, AC-I7 and AC-I10, but omit AC-I1 and the underlying I1 rule. Those inherited requirements still describe a successful integrity calculation as the condition for remembering a page and require its first read to calculate integrity. Q1 instead remembers a successfully transferred non-overflow page without calculating its integrity, and AC-Q1 requires its next read to perform no integrity calculation.

**Evidence:**

- The draft's acceptance preamble says: «Valen los de los ascendientes, que deben seguir pasando sin cambios salvo los que este PRD sustituye o precisa» (`:116`).
- Ancestor I1 says: «Solo se recuerdan comprobaciones superadas: una página que no la pasa nunca se recuerda» (`docs/prd/krummi/pager/pager-integrity-once.prd.md:38`).
- Ancestor AC-I1 requires: «la integridad de cada página y del valor se comprueba solo en su primera lectura» (`docs/prd/krummi/pager/pager-integrity-once.prd.md:150`, continuing at `:152`). Its preamble applies this to disk and temporary stores (`:143`).
- Q1 says the transferred pages «quedan recordadas como comprobadas, con ese código, en cuanto sus bytes quedan escritos en su sitio» (`docs/prd/krummi/pager/pager-written-pages.prd.md:38`). AC-Q1 then requires a new transaction to read the transferred leaf «sin calcular su integridad ni comprobar su organización» (`:125`, continuing at `:128`).
- The existing AC-I1 fixture creates and transfers a temporary store without reopening it (`crates/krummi/src/integrity_tests.rs:86`). This is a concrete inherited setup where the page-first-read expectation changes. Its aggregate `first > 0` assertion at `:93` also includes an off-leaf value, so this review does **not** claim that the current test necessarily fails.

**Observable readings:** preserving AC-I1 literally requires one integrity calculation on the first read of a freshly transferred tree page; following Q1 requires zero. Reopening a disk fixture can avoid the overlap, but a temporary store cannot reopen with its data preserved. The underlying I1 definition also needs the explicit distinction between verified contents and contents trusted because the engine wrote them.

**Suggestion:** declare Q1's exception to I1 and clarify AC-I1 for pages transferred during the current opening, preserving the existing rules for off-leaf values and pages not already remembered.

**Status:** open. This is a contract consistency correction; Q1 already states the product decision.
