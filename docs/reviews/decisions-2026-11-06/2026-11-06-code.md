**Location:** `docs/prd/krummi/pager-integrity-once.prd.md:192`; also its English translation at `docs/prd/krummi/pager-integrity-once.prd.en.md:188`.

O3 L16 identifies Linux W5 and W6 among the full-run drops, but the corresponding individual comparisons in O4 L10 cover only W2, W14, W15, W16 and W17 on Linux. The independently inspected `work/o4/full-wsl.out` contains exactly those five loads. **Concrete scenario and effect:** an acceptance auditor follows the annotation to determine whether Linux's concurrent-write drops were individually checked. The text presents them as measured non-regressions, although its supporting records do not contain those comparisons. The full reports show W5's displayed ratio falling from 2.30 to 2.08 and W6's from 10.45 to 9.60; this review does not establish their cause or a reproducible performance regression.

**Evidence type:** documentary/source argument, supported by independent parsing of the available measurement files and full benchmark reports. **Linked requirement:** AC-I12 and execution S12.

The user's explicit decision to accept AC-I12 and its amended noise rule remains authoritative. This finding concerns the newly asserted evidence coverage, not the validity of that product decision or the superseded acceptance rule. W16's mixed individual results are already disclosed as scatter and are not treated here as a demonstrated regression.

**Suggestion:** distinguish the accepted closure from the workloads actually measured, or supply the missing matching comparisons.

### R2 — minor — The 64 TiB annotation omits stores with a larger configured maximum

**Location:** `docs/prd/krummi/pager.prd.md:38`; also `docs/prd/krummi/pager.prd.en.md:40`.

The new annotation qualifies the ceiling with “sin tamaño máximo”, leaving the preceding configured-maximum case without the same qualification. The reservation applies to both cases: `file_pager` constructs `Remembered::new(MAX_FILE_PAGES, len / PAGE_SIZE_U64)` independently of `max_size` (`crates/krummi-pager/src/pager.rs:1050`), and `MAX_FILE_PAGES` covers 64 TiB (`crates/krummi-pager/src/remembered.rs:22`).

**Concrete scenario and causal chain:** open a disk store initially smaller than 64 TiB with a configured maximum of 128 TiB. That value passes option validation, but does not enlarge the remembered-page reservation. A subsequent growth beyond its coverage passes the configured-size check and reaches `grow` → `remember_up_to` → `Remembered::ensure`; the reservation-size check rejects it and maps the failure to `OutOfMemory`, before disk allocation (`pager.rs:723`, `:772`; `remembered.rs:103`). Configuring the larger maximum therefore does not permit growth past the accepted ceiling, which the amendment describes only for the unconfigured case.

**Evidence type:** verifiable technical argument through option validation, reservation and growth. No 64 TiB store was allocated. **Linked requirement:** P10 and I6/P13. This is incomplete documentation of the newly recorded ceiling, not a new engine defect introduced by these documentation commits.

**Suggestion:** state that the reservation ceiling also applies when the configured maximum exceeds 64 TiB.

**Status:** open.
