### R12 — Major: Cancellation still finishes buffered statistics construction

- Status: **still open, partially corrected** by `b93f241`. Row/leaf reads now observe shutdown between rows; the original finding's gathering/construction part remains.
- Location: `crates/krummi/src/table/statistics.rs:458`–`:473`, `:164`–`:197`; `crates/krummi/src/table/stats.rs:153`; `crates/krummi/src/table/worker.rs:268`.
- **Technical evidence:** for a large table, `sampled_rows` collects decoded rows and can return early on cancellation. Its caller unconditionally feeds that buffer into `build(rows.into_iter())`, which has no cancellation parameter. It processes all collected rows, encodes columns and index prefixes, then sorts/finalizes the gathers. Shutdown during this phase, or just before returning a substantial partial buffer because of shutdown, still waits for that work to complete; only then does `renew` check `stopping` and discard the result. Small-table gathering finalization also has no stop check. Thus cancellation can stop further row reads but does not abandon the active statistics construction as a whole.
- No shutdown latency was measured and no specific time bound or additional unbounded row reading is asserted. The cited close test holds `WorkSampled`, after construction, and cannot establish interruption during gathering.
- Requirement: S1, ST10: «cerrar detiene el muestreo en curso del hilo sin publicarlo ni guardarlo»; AC-ST7. This is the remaining construction work described in original R12.
- Suggestion: abandon buffered processing and gathering/finalization when shutdown is requested.

## Related non-blocking caveat

The new `Engine::write_unless` grants a free turn without consulting `stop`; inside `wait_turn`, an available turn is also checked before cancellation. A harness with `write_unless(&|| true)` and a free turn returned `Ok("acquired")`. The general race in which shutdown starts while a save is waiting and it subsequently acquires the turn also existed with the base's 20 ms polling, which could acquire before timing out without rechecking shutdown. It is recorded as a pre-existing caveat, not an additional blocking regression or an expansion of R6's verified FIFO correction.
