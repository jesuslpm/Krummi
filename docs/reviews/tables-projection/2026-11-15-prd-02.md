### R1 — Verified resolved: copied-row output ownership and lifetime

- Original severity: **major**.
- Original reference: `docs/reviews/tables-projection/2026-11-15-prd.md#R1`.
- Sections and IDs: Product decisions / PY3; Output; Acceptance criteria / AC-PY10.
- Resolution verified against the corrected PRD bytes recorded above.

The resolution records the user's choice of independently owned results. PY3 now explicitly requires, for copied rows, «una copia propia de la aplicación que puede vivir más que la fila copiada» (`tables-projection.prd.md:39`). Output distinguishes borrowed rows from copied rows (`:93–96`). AC-PY10 requires «su texto, sus bytes y sus vectores siguen valiendo después de soltar la fila copiada» (`:195–198`). Together these passages settle the ownership choice and provide an observable acceptance check that rejects results borrowing the copied row.

**Reproduction of the original evidence:** the earlier report supplied a source trace, rather than a compiled experiment. Repeating that trace confirms that `TableRow::into_owned` returns `TableRow<'static>` and materializes owned values (`crates/krummi/src/table/rows.rs:991–1001`). `value` clones the owned branch (`:943–962`); typed reads fall back through that branch (`:1050`). `Value` derives `Clone`, holds text/bytes/vectors in `Cow`, and converts them to owned contents in `into_owned` (`crates/krummi/src/table/value.rs:282–311`, `:336–340`). Text and bytes accessors preserve those owned results, including reading text as bytes (`rows.rs:1088–1096`, `:1148–1151`). Thus the resolution correctly describes existing positional access, and the revised requirement preserves it.

**Dependent invariants and regression sweep:** D11, tables Output and T8 still govern borrowed page data. Returning an independent copy from an explicitly copied row does not extend a page borrow or the lifetime of a projection; ordinary read/scanned rows retain their no-copy contract. Historical layouts and borrowed defaults are resolved while obtaining the row (`rows.rs:570–620`, `:663–720`), and `into_owned` materializes the resulting values before transaction changes (`:994`). The correction preserves wrong-schema rejection in AC-PY10 and does not alter panic, transaction, schema-identity or stored-format rules. No additional observable ownership reading remains in these corrected sections.
