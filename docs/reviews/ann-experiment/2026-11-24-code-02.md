### R2 — major — Complete the required memory measurement and distinguish payload estimates

**Status:** partially corrected, still open. Original ID: [2026-11-24-code.md#R2](2026-11-24-code.md#R2). Reviewed fixes: `96ee45487af604ebb0771b65c607a0a2a62f1d09` and `ffd260972ec3b815a78d31b1d3b1ebddb89ccf32`.

**Locations:** `docs/benchmark/2026-11-24-ann-experiment.md:206` and `:210`; propagated to `docs/design/tables/12-vector-indexes.md:588`. Dependent implementation: `crates/krummi-ann-lab/src/ivf.rs:154`, `:234`, and `crates/krummi-ann-lab/src/memory.rs:44`.

**Verified improvements:** the implementation now reads Linux `VmRSS`/`VmHWM`, reports before/after RSS and explicitly includes allocator-retained temporaries. The preserved Linux outputs contain observed measurements at 100,000 rows; all 14 printed RSS differences agree with their endpoints within rounding. The summary uses the separate dot-product process for IVF/HNSW instead of the known reused-process values. HNSW's four-byte-link figure is now correctly labelled a compact estimate, consistent with the locked dependency's shared pointers and vector copies. Those parts of the original finding are resolved.

**Remaining defect:** the new report again turns a payload estimate into an assertion about actual retained memory. It says, “El índice que queda es el del tamaño calculado.” The design annotation repeats that IVF “keeps its computed size afterwards.” `Ivf::bytes()` counts lengths of centroid values, IDs, codes and vector values. The live index also retains per-row `Vec<f64>` norms, centroid norms, quantizer arrays, vector metadata and unused capacity. `Ivf::build()` grows the list vectors without shrinking them. Freeing transformed construction inputs does not free these live allocations.

**Executed reproduction:** a temporary Rust harness linked against the freshly built lab library and called its public `Ivf::build` with 1,500 rows, 128 dimensions, `Metric::L2Squared`, `lists_for(1500) == 1`, `sample_ids(..., 21)` and k-means seed 33, matching the measurement defaults. Input component `i` was `(i * 37 % 65521) as f32 / 65521.0`. After construction returned:

```text
list rows=1500 ids=1500/2048 codes=192000/262144 vectors=192000/262144 norms=1500/2048
n=1500 d=128 nlist=1 calculated_combined=973024 live_list_logical_bytes=978000 live_list_capacity_bytes=1335296 ratio=1.372
```

The first line gives length/capacity in elements. `calculated_combined` is the sum returned by `Ivf::bytes()`; `live_list_capacity_bytes` sums capacities multiplied by element sizes for IDs, codes, vectors and norms. The lists alone retain **37.2% more allocated element storage** than the claimed final size, before counting centroid/quantizer storage or metadata. This is an allocation-capacity measurement, not an RSS measurement. The incorrect conclusion is new in the correction; the older payload-counting helper is inspected as its dependency rather than reported as a new unrelated defect.

The HNSW conclusion similarly exceeds the evidence: benchmark lines 206–209 subtract vector payload from a process RSS delta, call the remainder graph occupancy and extrapolate it to 1 M. The delta explicitly includes allocator-retained temporaries and can exclude allocations served from already resident free memory. Starting a fresh metric process removes reuse from an earlier metric, but does not isolate live graph allocations from its own construction temporaries or earlier preparation. `hnsw.rs:56` allocates transformed points before the RSS baseline at line 68. The observed delta is useful evidence; the derived 2.7–2.8 KiB of graph-only occupancy is not independently established by it.

**Coverage still missing:** benchmark line 217 explicitly leaves RSS at 1 M and on Windows unmeasured. S5 names 10,000/100,000/1 M scales and S6 requires RSS for the comparison; the full-scale memory gap identified by the original report remains. The safe-standard-library limitation explains the chosen implementation, but does not establish that external process measurement is unavailable. Page-cache working-set measurement remains explicitly deferred to the stored index, consistent with this experiment's in-memory scope; this review does not invent a stored-index implementation requirement.

**Concrete failure scenario:** a reader deciding between IVF and HNSW uses the new retained-memory claims and full-scale extrapolation as measured structure occupancy. The IVF capacity reproduction disproves its asserted final footprint, while the graph-only extrapolation mixes live storage with allocator effects. Actual full-scale RSS evidence is still absent. This undermines the memory comparison required by S6; it does not refute the earlier recall or latency measurements.

**Evidence:** executed public-API allocation reproduction, source tracing of live allocations and sampling boundaries, and inspection/arithmetic verification of preserved outputs. **Requirement:** S6, with S5's scales and the engineering principle to preserve correctness. Severity remains **major**, under the original R2 ID.

**Suggestion:** distinguish calculated payload, observed process RSS growth and retained allocations, correct the derived footprint claims, and complete the outstanding memory evidence before declaring R2 resolved.
