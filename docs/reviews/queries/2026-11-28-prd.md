## Findings

<a id="R1"></a>
### R1 — major — Unqualified statement recovery conflicts with transaction-fatal storage errors

**Location:** Reparto en PRD hijos / 17.7, `docs/prd/krummi/queries/queries.prd.md:130`. **Status:** open.

The write-query contract promises that every failed statement leaves the surrounding transaction able to commit its other changes:

> una sentencia que falla no deja ninguno de sus cambios y la transacción puede confirmar los demás, como la regla de B7 de los árboles.

That promise is broader than the cited rule. `docs/prd/krummi/trees/trees.prd.md:47` explicitly limits B7's recoverable behavior to public validation errors and preserves the invalidating effects of T11. `docs/prd/krummi/transactions/transactions.prd.md:40` specifies that disk-full, maximum-size, out-of-memory, damage and I/O failures within a write transaction prevent its commit:

> la transacción ya no puede confirmarse.

T12 (`transactions.prd.md:41`) additionally preserves the store-wide refusal of writes after a storage write I/O failure. The root's G7/G12 (`docs/prd/krummi/krummi.prd.md:175`, `:180`) retain the corresponding failure guarantees. The proposed annotations at `queries.prd.md:305` do not declare an override of those rules.

The current code implements this boundary: `crates/krummi/src/table/rows.rs:1390` distinguishes public validation errors, which permit commit, from other failures, which do not; `crates/krummi-tx/src/transaction.rs:630` rejects commit when a transaction failure was recorded.

**Observable example:** a write transaction changes row A through the existing API, then runs an `UPDATE` query that encounters a damaged page. Under the unqualified 17.7 promise, it can still commit the change to A. Under T11, its commit must fail and apply none of its changes.

**Readings allowed:** recover locally after every statement error, or recover locally only after errors that do not invalidate the transaction. These produce different persisted results and different transaction usability. The child cannot satisfy both contracts without an explicit boundary or override.

**Suggestion:** delimit the statement-local recoverable errors and expressly preserve T11/T12 for storage failures, or declare the intended exception to the existing guarantees.
