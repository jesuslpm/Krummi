### R1 — major — Unqualified statement recovery conflicts with transaction-fatal storage errors

**Previous finding:** `docs/reviews/queries/2026-11-28-prd.md#R1`. **Disposition: verified resolved.**

**Location:** Reparto en PRD hijos / 17.7, `docs/prd/krummi/queries/queries.prd.md:130`; references at `:360`. The reviewed hash matches the version claimed fixed in the resolutions.

The corrected contract expressly distinguishes public validation errors from transaction-fatal failures:

> una sentencia que falla con un error de validación pública (B7 de los árboles: valor no válido, clave duplicada, tamaño no válido) no deja ninguno de sus cambios y la transacción puede confirmar los demás; un fallo que T11 de las transacciones hace fatal para la transacción (disco lleno, tamaño máximo, memoria agotada, daño, entrada y salida) sigue siéndolo, y T12 sigue valiendo para el almacén.

This preserves T11/T12 (`docs/prd/krummi/transactions/transactions.prd.md:40`, `:41`), B7 and its table extension (`docs/prd/krummi/trees/trees.prd.md:47`, `:169`), N10 (`docs/prd/krummi/tables/tables.prd.md:53`) and root G7/G12 (`docs/prd/krummi/krummi.prd.md:175`, `:180`). No override of their failure guarantees is needed.

**Original reproduction, verified analytically:** a transaction changes row A, then an `UPDATE` encounters a damaged page. Under the corrected 17.7, damage remains fatal under T11: commit must fail and cannot persist A or any other change of that transaction. The two incompatible persisted outcomes in the original finding are eliminated. This is a contract/source verification, not an executed query reproduction; the query layer is not implemented.

**Dependent implementation evidence:** `crates/krummi/src/table/rows.rs:1390` distinguishes recoverable public validation from fatal failures; `:1511` and `:1523` return validation failures before applying row changes. `crates/krummi-tx/src/transaction.rs:1066` retains the first transaction failure, and `:630` rejects commit before publishing changes. A later validation error cannot clear that failure. `crates/krummi-tx/src/lib.rs:804` propagates the store's refusal of writes to waiting requests, preserving T12.

**Regression and gap sweep:** statement-wide rollback after a public validation failure remains required, including after earlier rows of that statement succeeded; the wording does not reduce it to per-row rollback or prescribe an internal mechanism. Budget exhaustion and cancellation are explicitly assigned to the *Fija* cell of 17.7. They do not silently override T11/T12. Query spill behavior remains subject to CS9 and the existing distinction in `transaction.rs:585`: temporary-sort errors become transaction failures when the higher layer records them as such. The correction introduces no blanket rule making every temporary-file failure fatal. The former product question about preserving versus changing T11/T12 is settled by expressly preserving them.
