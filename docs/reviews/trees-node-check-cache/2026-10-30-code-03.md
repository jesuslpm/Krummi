### R3 — verified resolved — AC-C8 hangs instead of reporting a reader or writer failure

Original severity: **minor**. Original finding: [2026-10-30-code-02.md#R3](2026-10-30-code-02.md#R3). Reviewed disposition: `fixed`, commit `8cf01cfba9b4a91e20f1e13a4c36fb460da3a7f1`.

**Source evidence:** `crates/krummi/src/check_tests.rs:416` introduces a guard inside the `thread::scope` closure. During writer-thread unwinding, its destructor publishes `failed = true` before the scope's automatic wait for readers. The reader loop at `:432` checks this flag independently of `done` and its minimum round count. Thus healthy readers can leave even while `done` remains false. A reader panic reaches the writer through `two_rounds` checking `is_finished()` (`:463`) or the explicit `join().unwrap()` (`:494`); either route activates the same guard. A writer panic activates it directly. This breaks the circular wait identified in R3.

**Independent reproduction:** the coordinator compiled the current `lib.rs` and `check_tests.rs` from stdin against Cargo-selected current test dependencies, inlining the checked test module and omitting unrelated test modules. Repository sources were never changed. The reader variant inserted the original report's once-only panic immediately before `let mut seen = BTreeSet::new();`. The writer variant panicked immediately after the third generation's commit. Each variant was run with the failure in the disk iteration and separately in the temporary iteration. Diagnostic prints observed the guard and reader exit; they did not change loop conditions or engine behavior.

All four executions produced `FAILED` and exit 101 within the 45-second safety bound. The reader failures printed `a reader stopped while the writer ran`, followed by cancellation and three healthy reader exits. The writer failures printed cancellation and four reader exits. In all failing iterations the exiting healthy readers reported `done=false; failed=true`, establishing that cancellation, rather than the normal success signal, ended them. Representative output:

```text

a reader stopped while the writer ran
review cancellation signalled during writer unwind
review reader exited: count=1; done=false; failed=true
```

Artifacts: `target/review-node-check-cache-r3-reader-panic.exe`, `target/review-node-check-cache-r3-writer-panic.exe`, and `target/review-node-check-cache-r3-{reader,writer}-{disk,temporary}.{stdout,stderr}.log`.
