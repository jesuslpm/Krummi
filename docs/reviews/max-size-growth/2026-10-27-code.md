## Findings

### R1 — major — Growth waits for transfer even when the maximum leaves ample room

**Location:** `crates/krummi-tx/src/transaction.rs:527` (new condition and synchronous flush at lines 527–529).

**Problem:** Every bounded-store growth with pending scratch versions now waits for the transfer thread. The condition does not check whether those versions would actually reduce the growth size. A slow transfer therefore blocks an otherwise independent reservation and keeps the writer's turn occupied, delaying subsequent writers too, even when the complete growth comfortably fits below the maximum.

**Concrete scenario and reproduction:** Open a temporary store with a 64 MiB maximum, pause background passes, fill the initial eight pages to the frontier and commit. The store plus scratch versions occupies 114,688 bytes. Hold a running transfer in its test hook and start another thread that reserves one page and commits. The full growth to 16 pages fits with ample room, but the writer cannot complete until that transfer is released. The coordinator reproduced this against head with a 750 ms observation window; the same probe with no maximum completed while the transfer remained held:

```text
bounded=false, before_bytes=114688, maximum=None, writer_completed_while_transfer_held=true, after_pages=16
bounded=true, before_bytes=114688, maximum=Some(67108864), writer_completed_while_transfer_held=false, after_pages=16
```

**Causal evidence:** `ScratchWriter::flush()` (`crates/krummi-pager/src/scratch.rs:842`) sends `Demand::Flush`. `Signal::demand()` (`transfer.rs:148`) waits for its answer. The single transfer thread serves the demand only after returning from its current work (`transfer.rs:291`). The new call happens inside the open write transaction before growth, while it still owns its turn. At the base, `reserve()` went directly to `ensure_pages_within()` when the reservation fit; in this scenario scratch would not cap the full doubling either. This is a reproduced dependency on transfer completion, not a claim of corruption or a production deadlock.

**Violated requirements:** Journal J5 requires transfer “sin bloquear a los lectores ni al escritor”. AC-J17 requires “entonces las dos terminan sin esperar al traslado”. J7/S1 requires transferring versions to prevent maximum-size failures; it does not require waiting when the versions do not constrain the growth. The implementation's accepted RDD warning in ODD L4 does not amend those product requirements. The grouped-journal PRD also preserves J5 explicitly.

**Coverage gap:** `ac_j17_a_running_transfer_blocks_neither_writers_nor_readers` (`crates/krummi-tx/tests/journal.rs:180`) uses an unbounded store and rewrites an existing page. The two new growth tests verify maximum-size accounting but never hold a running transfer while a bounded store with ample room grows. Consequently all existing tests pass despite this regression.

**Suggestion:** Restrict the pre-growth flush to cases where pending versions constrain growth sizing, and cover a bounded growth with ample room while a transfer is held.

No other demonstrated defect introduced by this range was found.
