| S2: a mapping covers the observed length | pager.rs:598–620 serializes growth, retains the mapping, publishes its pointer with Release, then publishes length with Release. raw_pages loads length with Acquire before loading the pointer. Observing a growth's length makes that growth's preceding pointer publication happen-before the pointer load; atomic coherence permits that pointer or a later one. Every later mapping covers at least that length. An older observed length is also safe. |

Moving Mmap handles does not move their mapped bytes. raw_pages checks addition, byte-count conversion and the page bound before pointer arithmetic; sys::map validates conversion of the mapped length to usize.
