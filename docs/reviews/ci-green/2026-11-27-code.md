### R1 — The pause helper does not acknowledge an already-dispatched transfer

- Severity: **minor**. Status: **pre-existing advisory; outside the requested introduced-defect scope; non-blocking**.
- Location: `crates/krummi-tx/tests/group_commit.rs:181`; unchanged helper implementation at `crates/krummi-pager/src/scratch.rs:593` and dispatch at `crates/krummi-pager/src/transfer.rs:212`.
- Problem: the added pause reduces interference with AC-E13 but does not prove that every possible transfer has stopped before fault injection.
- Concrete scenario: `Signal::next_work` selects `Work::Pass` and releases the signal mutex at `transfer.rs:239`. The worker is descheduled before `Shared::pass` takes `flushing` at `scratch.rs:763`. The test sets the pause flag, acquires and releases the still-free `flushing` mutex, returns from the pause and injects `Op::Write`. The previously dispatched transfer resumes, writes the earlier commit and can consume the injected panic. The journal writer can then complete the tested commit, causing `unwrap_err()` at `group_commit.rs:195` to fail.
- Evidence: source-based concurrency argument with reachable scheduling preconditions; not experimentally reproduced. The dispatch, pause and transfer implementations are identical at the base, whose test already allowed the transfer to consume the injected panic. This range narrows that existing window and introduces no new failing behavior.
- Requirement: AC-E13 requires a simulated failure of the journal-writing mechanism with commits waiting; it does not intend to fail the transfer instead.
- Suggestion: obtain a worker acknowledgement that any dispatched pass has completed before injecting the journal fault.
